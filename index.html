Homemade Chicken Adobo

Ingredients

  • Chicken (Thighs or Drumsticks) (1kg)
  • Soy Sauce (1⁄2 cup)
  • Vinegar (1⁄2 cup)
  • Garlic (1 whole bulb)
  • Onion (1 medium(optional))
  • Bay Leave(Laurel) (3-4 pieces)
  • Whole black peppercorns (1 tsp)
  • Water (1 cup)
  • Cooking oil (2 tbsp)
  • Brown sugar (1 tsp (optional))

Kitchenwares

  • Cooking Pot
  • Spatula
  • Kitchen Knife
  • Chopping Board
  • Measuring Cups and Spoons
  • Kitchen Tongs
  • Mixing Bowls
  • Colander or Strainer

Other useful equipment

  • Stove or induction cooker
  • Ladle
  • Plate or serving bowl
  • Peeler(optional)
  • Garlic crusher(optional)

Cooking Procedures

Step 1: Marinate the chicken

  • Place the chicken in a mixing bowl.
  • Add soy sauce and half of the garlic.
  • Mix thoroughly.
  • Let it marinate for 15–30 minutes in the refrigerator.

Step 2: Sauté the aromatics

  • Heat cooking oil in a pot.
  • Add the remaining garlic and onion.
  • Sauté until fragrant and slightly golden.

Step 3: Brown the chicken

  • Remove the chicken from the marinade and reserve the marinade.
  • Add chicken to the pot.
  • Cook for a few minutes on each side until lightly browned.

Step 4: Add the seasonings

  • Pour in the reserved marinade.
  • Add vinegar, water, bay leaves, and peppercorns.
  • Bring to a boil.

Important:

Once you add the vinegar, let it boil uncovered for about 2–3 minutes before stirring.

Step 5: Simmer the chicken

  • Lower the heat.
  • Cover the pot and simmer for 25–35 minutes.
  • Occasionally check the chicken and turn the pieces.
  • Make sure the chicken is fully cooked.

Step 6: Adjust the sauce

  • Taste the sauce.
  • Add brown sugar if you prefer a slightly sweeter flavor.
  • If the sauce is too salty, add a little water.
  • If you want a thicker sauce, simmer uncovered for another 5–10 minutes.

Step 7: Serve

  • Transfer the chicken to a serving plate.
  • Pour the sauce over the chicken.
  • Serve with freshly cooked white rice.

Important cooking tips

  1. Don't rush the simmering process. This allows the chicken to absorb the flavors.
  2. Avoid adding too much soy sauce at the beginning. You can always adjust the seasoning later.
  3. Make sure the chicken reaches an internal temperature of 74°C (165°F).
  4. Refrigerate leftovers within two hours of cooking.
